# Admin Panel <!-- order: 3 -->

Everything you need to know about the admin panel and how it operates!

---

## Admin Pages

The admin panel is your home for managing all of your comics, posts, media, and users!

### Manage Posts

#### Dashboard

This is the main page of your admin panel. It displays some relevant information and stats about your app and comic. You can also find quick access to your most recent posts in each post type.

> [!TIP]
> This page and others will be more customizable at a later time!

#### Series

This is where you can manage your series details. Eventually, this will be able to support multiple series, so filling-in these details is optional at this time. Branding provided to this section can be used to replace the main site branding in the **Global Settings** page.

#### Comic Updates

This is where you can manage your comic posts! Updates serve as your main post type and are the main way to share your comic using this app.

> [!NOTE]
> Since the term "pages" is fairly generic and may cause confusion when referencing other aspect of this app, we have opted to refer to comic pages as "updates," generally.

The displayed table allows basic sorting of your posts and the action buttons allow you to view, edit, share, and delete the specific item.

#### Chapters

This is where you can manage your comic chapters! You may choose to create chapters, as well as **volumes**, before creating comic updates to ease the process.

#### Volumes

This is where you can manage your comic chapters! You may choose to create volumes, as well as **chapters**, before creating comic updates to ease the process.

#### Cast

This is where you can manage your cast and charaters that appear in your comic! These have a ton of preset options, but we know there's a LOT to say about them, so keep your eyes peeled for updates to this section!

#### Blogs

Here, you can manage and create new blog posts. These are mainly text-based posts to give information about new projects, life, events, whatever you want!

#### Gallery

This is where you can create posts to show off the cool artwork of your comic and characters, whether it was official or fan-made!

---

### Manage Assets

#### Media Files

This is your media library. Here, you can view, delete, copy a reference to, or otherwise manage your media files.

We currently only support images and audio!

---

### Site Settings

#### Global Settings

Settings found in this page affect your entire site and enable experimental features.

#### Themes

This is where you can manage your site's color themes! We use Skeleton UI's provided design system and theme format to keep site colors consistent, as well as easy to access and modify. Create, edits and delete custom themes!

#### App Pages

This is where you can manage your app pages. Hide, reorder, toggle link visibility, and more!

---

### User Accounts

#### Staff & Users

Here, you can manage staff and contributor details. Create new profiles, view details, and delete accounts!

#### My Account

A simple page for viewing your own account details and how they might appear to your readers.

#### Account Settings

A direct link to editing your own account details.

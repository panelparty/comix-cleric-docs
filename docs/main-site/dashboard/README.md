# Dashboard <!-- order: 2 -->

This page allows you to manage your account settings, reset your password, change your email, host and manage Comix Cleric sites, and register comics to appear on the front page.

---

## Hosted Sites

A list of site plans you are currently subscribed to. Each active plan will be charged a recurring amount every month, unless it is on a custom plan.

#### Title

A cosmetic title for the site plan. May include special characters.

#### Sources

Displays relevant links to the active plan's website home page and admin panel.

#### Status

Shows a green or red indicator and label to let you know the server is currently running for that specific site.

#### Plan

Displays the plan preset name and tooltip containing a short description of what's included.

#### Manage Plan

Contains two links, one to view/manage the current plan, and one to edit its details.

---

## Redeem Code

This page contains a single input for redeeming cool stuff for your account (eventually)!

> [!TIP]
> If you need to apply a discount code, this is not the place to do it. Discount codes are applied during checkout in the Stripe payment form, accessed after creating a new app plan.

---

## Account Settings

Shows you current account details and allows you to change them at your discretion.

### Edit Details

Allows you to alter certain aspects of your accounts, such as your avatar image, displayname, and/or username!

##### Displayname

A cosmetic name that is used in place of your `username`. It may contain spaces and special characters.

> Custom HTML and some special characters will not be rendered.

##### Username

A unique name that is used to differentiate accounts. This may not contain spaces, but may contain letters, periods, & numbers.

### Change Email

Allows you to add a new email address to replace your current one.

##### Email

The email address used to contact you. Hidden by default, click the icon on the right hand side to display.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> You must still have access to your currently registered email address to successfully change it without assistance!

### Change Password

Allows you to replace your current password by sending you a confirmation email that allows you access to securely enter and confirm a new password.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> You must still have access to your currently registered email address to successfully change your password without assistance!

### Connected Accounts

Shows you links to edit your account's connections to external services, like Discord.

##### Manage Discord Access

Takes you to the [Discord Access](#discord-access) page to manage your Discord connection there.

### Account Controls

Allows you to take actions regarding your account.

##### Logout

Sign you out of your current login session.

##### Delete Account

Allows you to delete your entire ComixCleric.com account, assuming you've cancelled any active plans and shut down your servers.

> [!NOTE]
> This process may automate cancellations and shut-downs at a later date.

---

## Discord Access

Shows you your active Discord account connection that allows you to sync any redeemed rewards that are relevant to Discord, such as badges and roles.

### No Connection:

#### Sign in with Discord

Opens an authentication session to connect your currently logged-in Discord account to your ComixCleric.com account.

### Active Connection:

#### Disconnect

Clears the connection between your ComixCleric.com account and your Discord account.

#### Current Connection

Displays the username of the connected Discord account for your visual verification.

#### Reload Roles

Clicking this button tries to sync your current ComixCleric.com account rewards to your Discord account, if applicable.

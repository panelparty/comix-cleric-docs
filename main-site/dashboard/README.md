# Creator Dashboard <!-- order: 4 -->

This page allows you to manage your account settings, reset your password, change your email, host and manage Comix Cleric sites, and register comics to appear on the front page.

---

### Account Details

###### Edit Details

Allows you to alter certain aspects of your accounts, such as your avatar image, displayname, and/or username!

###### Change Email

Allows you to add a new email address to replace your current one.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> You must still have access to your current email address to successfully change it without assistance!

###### Change Password

Allows you to replace your current password.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> You must still have access to your current email address to successfully change your password without assistance!

###### Account Status

Determines your account standing. Only "Active" accounts can create and manage plans, or register comics.

- **Inactive** - Your account has not been activated or verified yet.
- **Active** - Your account is fully operational.
- **Suspended** - Your account has been temporarily restricted.
- **Banned** - Your account has been permanently banned and may be deleted at a later time.
- **Deactivated** - Your account has been voluntarily deactived and can can be re-enabled at a later time.

###### Badge (Coming Soon)

A cosmetic label to display on your account. Only one can be active at a time.

###### Avatar

An image that represents your profile. Will be shown publicly.

###### Displayname

A cosmetic name that is used in place of your `username`. It may contain spaces and special characters.

> Custom HTML will not be rendered.

###### Username

A unique name that is used to differentiate accounts. This may not contain spaces, but may contain letters, periods, & numbers.

###### Email

The email address used to contact you. Hidden by default, click the icon on the right hand side to display.

---

### Hosted Sites

A list of site plans you are currently subscribed to. Each active plan will be charged a recurring amount every month, unless it is on a custom plan.

###### Title

A cosmetic title for the site plan. May include special characters.

###### Source

Displays a link to the active plan site instance.

###### Plan

Displays the plan preset name and short description of the site instance.

###### Manage Plan

Takes you to the management page for that specific site plan.

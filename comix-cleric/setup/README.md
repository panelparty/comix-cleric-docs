# First-time Setup <!-- order: 2 -->

This page contains information about setting up your Comix Cleric app for the first time!

---

> [!NOTE]
> If you are using our hosting plans, you should receive an email with instructions on setting up your new site! Simply follow the link, fill in a few details, and you're good to go!


### Setup

You can locate your setup page by adding `/api/setup` to your site URL in the addressbar.

The end result should look something like: `https://my-app.comixcleric.com/api/setup`

Here, you will be asked to fill-in a few details to create your local account. This account differs from your main ComixCleric.com site account. This new admin account is local to your site and gives you full access to modify and update your site appearance and create/update/delete posts.

#### Creating Your Admin Account:

Once you're on the setup page, provide the following information:

##### Email

-# *Required*

A valid email address. Used strictly for login and account notifications.

-# Max: 64 characters

##### Username

-# *Required; Displayed publicly*

A unique name for your profile. Similar to a "handle."

-# Min: 3, Max: 24 characters

##### Display Name

-# *Displayed publicly*

A custom cosmetic name for your profile.

-# Min: 3, Max: 32 characters

##### Password & Confirmation

-# *Required*

Secure password. Both values must be the same, and match the standard pattern:
-# Min: 8, Max: 64, Must contain at least 1 uppercase and lowercase letter, number, and special character *\!\@\#\$\%\^\&\**

##### Policy Agreement

-# *Required*

A checkbox aknowledging that you have read and agree to our policies. <u>**Must be checked to complete registration.**</u>

Once you have successfully registered a new account, an email should be sent to the address you provided. Follow the instructions in the email to verify your account. This should merely require clicking or copy/pasting a link into your browser's address bar.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> For issues, please request support via Discord.

#### Login

To access your newly-created or existing account, you need to access a login form. You'll have to try accessing the Admin Panel at `/_cc_admin`. You should find a login button that will allow you to enter your admin credentials and access the panel.

##### Email or Username

-# *Required*

The email address or username you used during registration.

##### Password

-# *Required*

The password you provided during registration.

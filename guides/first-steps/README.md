# Creating Your Account & First Hosted Site

This guide covers your first steps when using Comix Cleric for the first time.

---

### Creating Your Comix Cleric (Hub Site) Account:

> [!IMPORTANT]
> This guide assumes you've already retrieved your 8-digit (####-####) code from the official Kickstarter email.

1. Visit [comixcleric.com](https://comixcleric.com) (you should already be here).

2. Click on the **"Log In / Sign Up"** button. On Desktop, this is in the bottom-left corner of the page. On Mobile, it is in the menu on the top-left of your screen.

3. Click on the **"Register"** toggle (might already be set to register) and fill out the required basic info to create an account. Don't forget to save or write down your account info somewhere!

4. Click the **"Register"** button to send a verification email to the address you listed.

5. Check your email (and spam folders) for the Verification Email from `support@comixcleric.com` and click the **"Verify Email"** button to verify your email. (NOTE: This will bring you back to comixcleric.com in a new tab, but you may not receive confirmation this was successful at this time.)

6. Log in with your registered email and password. If everything is successful, you should now logged in!

---

### Creating a New "Site Plan":

1. Log into your account at [comixcleric.com](https://comixcleric.com).

2. Go to your Creator Dashboard, which you can find in the Navigation Menu on the top-left.

3. Click on the **"New Site"** or **"New Site Plan"** button to start the site creation process.

4. Choose a plan type and fill out your basic site info. **Note that you will NOT be charged, or even brought to any payment processing sites, during Early Access.** Additionally, custom domains are not available to use at this time (but will be available in the future).

5. Click the **"Add New Plan"** button to create your site. You will be brought to a dashboard that has your current site info and statistics. It may take a few minutes to be created, so please be patient!

6. Once you refresh the page (or return to the Dashboard and click Manage Site for your new site) and see that your site is online, your site has been successfully created!

---

### Set Up and Customize Your New Site:

1. After successfully creating your new site, check your email for a Site Setup Link from `support@comixcleric.com`. (Note: Some email services remove styles and links from our emails, Yahoo is one of them.)

2. Clicking the Site Setup Link will bring you to a page where you will set up your Admin Panel account. (Note that this is separate from your Comix Cleric account and will be unique for each site. Don't share your login info!)

3. Once your account is created, you may log in and access the Admin Panel for your site. Here, you can edit the Series details, create Comic Updates, Chapters, and/or Volumes, add Media Files, adjust Themes & Branding, and more!

4. Feel free to explore, adjust settings, and do whatever you'd like to try it out and help us test things! You can click the "Return to Main Site" button to see how your site currently looks and feels, or open it in a new tab to see the changes you make without leaving the Admin Panel.

---

### To Access Exclusive Discord Channels:

1. Log into your account at [comixcleric.com](https://comixcleric.com) and find your Creator Dashboard link in the sidebar.

2. Click on the **"Discord Access"** link that appears in the sidebar.

3. Click the **"Sign in with Discord"** button, which will allow you to connect your Discord account to your Comix Cleric account.

4. Once connected, you should be able to access exclusive channels in the Comix Cleric Discord Server, as well as a special server role, automatically! (If your roles don't automatically apply in Discord, please press the **"Reload Roles"** button once.)
